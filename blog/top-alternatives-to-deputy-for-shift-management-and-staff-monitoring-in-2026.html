Top Alternatives to Deputy for Shift Management and Staff Monitoring in 2026

In the fast-paced world of hospitality, managing shifts and monitoring staff can be a daunting task. Deputy has long been a popular choice for shift management and staff monitoring, but with new competitors entering the market, it's worth exploring other options that might better suit your needs.

This guide will compare some of the top alternatives to Deputy, including Connecteam, Jibble, Clockify, Toggl Track, and more. We'll delve into their features, pricing, and user reviews to help you find the most suitable software for your scheduling and monitoring requirements.

Why Consider Alternatives to Deputy?

While Deputy is a robust tool, it might not be the perfect fit for every business. Some common reasons for looking at alternatives include:

  • Cost: Deputy's pricing can be steep for small businesses or startups.
  • Feature Set: You might need specific features that Deputy doesn't offer.
  • User Experience: The interface and usability of Deputy might not align with your team's preferences.

Top Alternatives to Deputy

1. Connecteam

Features:

  • Shift Scheduling: Easy-to-use scheduling tools for creating and managing shifts.
  • Time Tracking: Accurate time tracking with GPS check-in/out features.
  • Communication Tools: In-app messaging and announcements to keep the team informed.

Pricing: Connecteam offers a free plan with basic features, and paid plans start at $39/month for up to 20 users.

User Reviews: Highly praised for its user-friendly interface and comprehensive feature set.

2. Jibble

Features:

  • Time Tracking: Real-time tracking of work hours with GPS check-ins.
  • Shift Management: Simple shift scheduling and management tools.
  • Reports: Detailed reports on attendance, overtime, and more.

Pricing: Free for up to five users; paid plans start at $2.99/user/month.

User Reviews: Users appreciate the ease of use and the detailed reporting features.

3. Clockify

Features:

  • Time Tracking: Manual and automatic time tracking.
  • Shift Scheduling: Basic shift scheduling capabilities.
  • Reports: Comprehensive reports on work hours, attendance, and productivity.

Pricing: Free for basic features; paid plans start at $4.99/user/month.

User Reviews: Users love the detailed reporting and the ability to track time manually or automatically.

4. Toggl Track

Features:

  • Time Tracking: Real-time tracking of work hours.
  • Shift Management: Basic shift scheduling tools.
  • Reports: Detailed reports on productivity and attendance.

Pricing: Free for up to five users; paid plans start at $9/user/month.

User Reviews: Users appreciate the simplicity and accuracy of time tracking.

5. Huddle

Huddle is a specialized shift management app designed specifically for hospitality venues like cafés, bars, pubs, bakeries, and bistros. It offers a range of features that make it an excellent alternative to Deputy:

  • GPS Check-in: Ensures employees clock in only when they are physically at the business location.
  • Shift Planning: Build weekly schedules quickly from one dashboard.
  • Shift Swap: Allows employees to swap shifts with manager approval.
  • Team Chat: Centralizes all work communication within the app.
  • Push Notifications: Instantly notify team members of shift changes and announcements.
  • Reports: Provides at-a-glance reports on lateness, attendance, and shifts.
  • Huddle AI: Automatically drafts next week's rota based on historical data and employee availability.

Pricing: Currently available for iOS (download from the App Store); Android version coming soon. Pricing details will be announced upon release.

User Reviews: Early users praise Huddle for its intuitive interface and powerful scheduling tools tailored to hospitality needs.

Choosing the Right Tool

When selecting a shift management and staff monitoring tool, consider the following factors:

  • Budget: Determine how much you can afford to spend on software.
  • Feature Set: Identify which features are essential for your business operations.
  • User Experience: Ensure the tool is user-friendly for both managers and employees.
  • Scalability: Choose a solution that can grow with your business.

Conclusion

Finding the right shift management and staff monitoring tool can significantly improve your operational efficiency and team satisfaction. Whether you choose Connecteam, Jibble, Clockify, Toggl Track, or Huddle, each of these alternatives offers unique features that cater to different needs.

If you're in the hospitality industry, Huddle is designed specifically for venues like cafés, bars, pubs, bakeries, and bistros. Its GPS check-in, shift planning, team chat, and AI-driven scheduling make it a powerful tool for managing your team effectively.
