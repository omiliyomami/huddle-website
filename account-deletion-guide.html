Account & Data

Here's how to delete your account and data.

Deleting your account removes your profile and associated data from Huddle. Follow the three steps below.

1

Go to Profile

Open the Huddle app and tap the Profile tab.

2

Tap Delete Account

Scroll to the bottom of Profile and tap Delete Account, then confirm.

3

Your account is deleted

Your account is deactivated immediately and you're signed out. Your data is then permanently erased.

About your data

Your profile and personal data are permanently removed. Some records may be retained for a short period where required by law or for security and fraud prevention, then deleted.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
