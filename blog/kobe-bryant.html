Kobe Bryant

Kobe Bryant was an American professional basketball player who played his entire 20-year career with the Los Angeles Lakers of the National Basketball Association (NBA). He is widely considered one of the greatest basketball players of all time.

Achievements

  • 5-time NBA champion
  • 18-time All-Star
  • 2-time NBA Finals MVP
  • 2-time Olympic gold medalist
